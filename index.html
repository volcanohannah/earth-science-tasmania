Working Together

Earth Science Tasmania brings together leading Earth science education organisations across the state. By collaborating, we strengthen Earth science education and outreach, creating meaningful opportunities for students, teachers, and community members to connect with the fascinating geology and landscapes that make Tasmania unique.
